Our Story

Where Every Meal Tells a Story

Born from a passion for authentic Tamil flavors

Kurinji Restaurant was founded with a simple dream — to bring the authentic, heartwarming flavors of Tamil Nadu to every table. Named after the Kurinji flower that blooms once in twelve years, we believe every meal with us is a rare and memorable experience.

Our chefs have perfected age-old recipes from the hills and coastline of South India, from aromatic Chettinad curries to crispy dosas and rich biryanis.
