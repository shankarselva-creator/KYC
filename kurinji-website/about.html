Named After a Rare Flower

The Kurinji flower blooms only once every twelve years in the Nilgiri hills of Tamil Nadu — a rare and beautiful event that locals celebrate with great joy. We named our restaurant after this flower because we believe that every meal here should be equally special and unforgettable.

Founded in 2010 by Chef Murugan Krishnaswamy, a native of Chettinad, Kurinji Restaurant was born from a deep desire to share the extraordinary flavours of his homeland with the world. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into one of the most beloved South Indian restaurants in the region.

Every dish on our menu is rooted in authentic recipes that have been passed down through generations, yet prepared with the freshest local ingredients. We believe that great food has the power to bring people together — and that belief is at the heart of everything we do.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the Kurinji story

2010

The Beginning

Chef Murugan opens Kurinji in a small 30-seat restaurant with a simple menu of dosas, idlis and curries. Word spreads fast about the authentic flavours.

2013

First Award

Kurinji wins "Best South Indian Restaurant" at the Regional Food Awards. The team doubles in size to meet growing demand.

2016

Expansion

We move to our current larger premises, adding a private dining room and expanding the kitchen to accommodate the full Chettinad menu.

2019

Catering Launch

Kurinji Catering is launched, bringing our food to weddings, corporate events and private parties across the region.

2022

Community Recognition

Voted "Favourite Family Restaurant" by local readers for the third consecutive year. A 5-star hygiene rating maintained throughout.

2025

Today & Beyond

Celebrating 15 years of excellence and looking forward to serving the next generation of Kurinji lovers. New seasonal menu launching soon.
